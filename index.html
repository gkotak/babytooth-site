What We Offer

Caring for every little smile

01
First Dental Visit

Welcoming babies from their very first tooth — around age one. A gentle, fun introduction designed to build lifelong trust with the dentist.

02
Preventive Care & Cleanings

Regular check-ups, professional cleanings, fluoride treatments, and sealants to keep tiny teeth cavity-free and healthy.

03
Sedation Dentistry

Safe, compassionate care using laughing gas, oral conscious sedation, or general anesthesia — for anxious children or complex cases.

04
Laser Dentistry

Minimally invasive laser treatments — faster healing, far less discomfort, and no scary drills for soft tissue procedures.

05
Tongue & Lip Ties

Expert assessment and gentle laser frenectomy from newborns to teens — supporting feeding, speech, and development.

06
Hospital Dentistry

Comprehensive treatment under general anesthesia for children with complex medical needs or significant dental anxiety.

Meet Dr. Nidhi

Globe-trotting mom.
World-class dentist.

Dr. Nidhi Kotak is a dual American & Canadian board-certified Consultant Paediatric Dentist — one of very few practitioners in the world to hold both credentials simultaneously. With over 15 years of international experience across the UAE, USA, Canada, India, and Kenya, she brings a truly global perspective to every child's care.

A certified lactation counselor, published researcher, and educator who has taught over 90 general dentists across British Columbia, Dr. Nidhi is also a trained improv comedian (Second City, Chicago) — because she believes laughter is the best behaviour management tool there is.

As a mom herself, she understands exactly what parents in Dubai need: a dentist who is clinically exceptional, culturally fluent, and genuinely warm.
